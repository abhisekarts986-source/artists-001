Art Introduction

🎨 Art Introduction

Welcome to the world of art! Art is a creative way to express thoughts, feelings, and imagination. Anyone can learn art with regular practice and patience.

Types of Art

• Sketching
• Portrait Drawing
• Painting
• Digital Art
• Cartoon & Illustration

Why Learn Art?

Art improves creativity, observation skills, focus, and confidence. It can also become a profession through commissions, freelancing, and content creation.

How to Start?

Start with basic shapes, lines, and simple objects. Practice daily, learn shading techniques, and gradually move to portrait drawing.

Beginner Guide

✏️ Beginner Guide

Learn Basic Shapes
Start with circles, squares, triangles, and rectangles. Most drawings are built from these shapes.
: Practice Lines
Practice straight lines, curved lines, and different patterns to improve hand control.
Learn Shading
Learn light and dark values using pencils. Shading makes drawings realistic.
Draw Simple Objects
Draw simple objects like apples, cups, books, and bottles before moving to complex subjects.
Start Face Drawing
Learn facial proportions and practice eyes, nose, lips, and ears separately.
Art Materials Guide

🛠️ Art Materials Guide

✏️ Drawing Pencils

HB, 2B, 4B, 6B, 8B pencils are commonly used for sketching and shading.

📒 Sketchbook

A sketchbook helps artists practice and save their artwork.

🩹 Eraser

Use normal and kneaded erasers for corrections and highlights.

📏 Grid Size Guide

A4 Paper (8.3 × 11.7 inch)

✔ Beginner: 1 cm × 1 cm grid
✔ Portrait: 2 cm × 2 cm grid

A3 Paper (11.7 × 16.5 inch)

✔ Beginner: 2 cm × 2 cm grid
✔ Large Portrait: 3 cm × 3 cm grid

Chart Paper

✔ Small Drawing: 3 cm × 3 cm grid
✔ Large Drawing: 5 cm × 5 cm grid

Grid Method Tips

  • Small details = Small grid
  • Large portrait = Large grid
  • Use ruler for accurate lines
  • Draw grid lightly so it can be erased later
